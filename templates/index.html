A Pioneer in Mathematics

Harish-Chandra (11 October 1923 – 16 October 1983) was an Indian-born mathematician who made fundamental contributions to representation theory, harmonic analysis and the theory of Lie groups.

Born in Kanpur, India, he initially studied physics before pursuing advanced research in mathematics. His work transformed the study of representations of semisimple Lie groups and continues to influence modern mathematics.

His scientific journey took him from India to Cambridge, Princeton and Columbia University, and ultimately to the Institute for Advanced Study in Princeton.

His Legacy

Harish-Chandra's legacy is not limited to awards or academic appointments. His mathematical methods and theories continue to help researchers understand the structure and representations of Lie groups.
